## About

Master's researcher in Electronics Engineering at **Jeju National University**, based in
Jeju, South Korea on the **Global Korea Scholarship**. Before the lab, six years of
full-stack engineering — APIs, dashboards, automation, and the unglamorous data
plumbing that keeps them fed.

That combination is the point. Most edge-ML work fails somewhere between the sensor
and the screen, and I have spent enough time on both ends to build the whole path
rather than a slice of it.

Currently going deep on **Edge AI and TinyML**, **computer vision**, and
**time-series anomaly detection**, applied to smart agriculture, environmental
monitoring, energy management, and industrial safety.
